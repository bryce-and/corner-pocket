# Corner Pocket

A free 3D pool game (8-ball and 9-ball) that runs in your phone's browser. No install, no account.

**Play:** https://bryce-and.github.io/corner-pocket/

## How to play

- **Aim:** drag across the table. Hold the arrow buttons for fine aim. Pinch to zoom.
- **Shoot:** pull the power bar on the right down, then let go.
- **Spin:** touch anywhere on the cue ball icon: high for follow, low for draw, left or right for english.
- **Top view:** tap anywhere on the table to aim straight at that spot.

## Rules

**8-ball:** the table is open after the break. Sink a ball to claim solids or stripes. Clear your group, then sink the 8 to win. Sinking the 8 early loses.

**9-ball:** always hit the lowest-numbered ball first. Sink the 9 on a legal shot to win.

In both, a scratch, a miss, or hitting the wrong ball first gives your opponent ball in hand.

## Modes

- **Play the CPU**
- **2 Players** on one phone (pass it between shots)
- **Play online:** send a friend a link and play on two phones
- **Practice:** clear the rack in as few shots as you can

Built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/). The whole game is the single `index.html` file.
